About Portal

Campus Event Management & Registration Portal is a centralized digital platform developed for students, faculty and event organizers at Lovely Professional University. The portal simplifies event discovery, registration and participation for technical competitions, workshops, conferences, cultural programs, sports activities and university events. Students can explore upcoming events, register online and access useful resources through one platform.
